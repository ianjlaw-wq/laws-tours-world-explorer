Controls

Touchscreen

Left thumb: joystick to walk. Push it further to run.

Right side: swipe to look around. Pinch to zoom.

JUMP button to jump. TALK / EXAMINE button to interact.

📖 opens the Explorer's Journal. CC = subtitles, 🔊 = Killa's voice, ⚙ = pause.

Keyboard & mouse

W A S D / arrow keys to walk. Hold Shift to run.

Space to jump.

Drag the mouse to look around. Scroll wheel to zoom.

E to talk / examine. J for the Journal. Esc closes panels.

Turn your phone sideways for the best view ↻
